Privacy Policy

Back Porch Games
Effective date: November 12, 2026
Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Back Porch Games (“we”, “us”) handles information in the mobile games we publish, including Block Brunch: Block Puzzle and Big Dill: Idle Pickle Tycoon, and any other games we publish under the Back Porch Games name (the “Games”).

The short version

Our Games do not collect, store, or share your personal information. Your game progress stays on your device. The only outside party involved is Google AdMob, which serves the optional rewarded ads in our Games and may collect advertising-related information as described below.

Information we collect

We collect no personal information. The Games do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, or any other personal details, and we operate no accounts, sign-ins, or servers that store your data.

Game data — such as scores, ranks, settings, and saved runs — is stored only on your device. Uninstalling a Game deletes that data from your device.

Advertising

Our Games show rewarded ads — ads you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game benefit. Ads are served by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service.

AdMob may collect information to serve and measure ads, which can include your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, device and operating system information, approximate location, and information about how you interact with ads. This information is collected and used by Google under its own privacy policy, not by us.

Learn more:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google My Ad Center — where you can control ad personalization

You can also reset or limit your device’s advertising identifier in your device settings (Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Analytics

We do not currently use analytics in our Games. If we add an analytics service in a future update, we will update this policy before that version is released.

Accounts and sign-in

The Games have no accounts and no sign-in. There is nothing to register for and no profile that identifies you.

Children’s privacy

Our Games are intended for general audiences and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us at the address below and we will help remove it — though, as described above, our Games do not collect personal information in the first place.

Data retention and deletion

Because we collect no personal information and operate no servers holding your data, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our side. All game data lives on your device and is removed when you uninstall the Game. Advertising data held by Google is governed by Google’s privacy policy and your Google ad settings.

Third-party links

The Games do not link out to other websites, except as part of advertising served by AdMob. This policy does not cover third-party services; please review their policies directly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if we add analytics or new features. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the Games after a change means you accept the updated policy.
